# Member module surfaces

Operate mode, ordinary extensions of the current rounded burgundy/Readex Pro app.
Targets: profiles/[id], account avatar, friends, tournaments/[id], history, leaderboard.

WORLD: Existing DESIGN.md and source tokens are visual authority. Preserve rose/ivory and wine themes, rounded controls and the existing board's motion/geometry. No new palette, fonts, illustration or visual-world replacement.
THESIS: Members find each other, organize one four-person bracket and understand saved results through compact forms and readable lists.
TOPOLOGY: Profiles lead with identity/actions, friends with search/relationships, tournaments with registration then fixed semifinal-to-final progression, history with summary then matches and standings link. On mobile, the bracket stacks in semifinal/semifinal/final order.
FIRST VIEWPORT: One descriptive heading and the main action or current result. Name, avatar and explicit relationship/status text carry identity. No decorative metric hero, nested card grids or hidden primary actions.
FORM: Shared semantic form/table/pagination primitives, normal member lists and a fixed bracket. Code-led extension of the approved incumbent world; concept-seed is not applicable under new-work §3, "Extend an existing surface": inherit its world and composition, with no concept tournament. No concept roll is claimed.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Preserve privacy, en/fr/ar/RTL, both themes, 360px mobile and 320px navigation. Empty, pending, failure, stale-action, draw-replay, cancelled and champion states must be explicit. Runtime user-uploaded avatars are user data, not generated decorative assets. DESIGN.md remains unchanged unless an approved system change actually ships.
